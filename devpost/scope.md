# Pakka

One line: A private, on-device evidence binder that helps renters prepare a dated record of their deposit and move-out condition.

## The Unique Kernel
WhatsApp confirmation is Pakka's distinctive step: it turns a spoken term into a written record. Pakka also brings the renter's room-condition and payment evidence together on a dated timeline for a move-out pack.

## Who It's For
Students and young renters in India living in a PG or rented room without a written agreement, who worry their landlord may keep the deposit.

## The Core Loop
The renter records a spoken term, opens WhatsApp with a written confirmation ready, adds a room photo and payment screenshot, then builds a move-out pack from the timeline.

## Inspiration & Identity
Not established.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
The learner wants a reliable plan so the AI remembers decisions and avoids repeating mistakes.

## What "Working" Looks Like
In a demo, a renter adds a spoken term, confirms it through WhatsApp, adds a room photo and sees its fingerprint, adds a payment screenshot, and builds a move-out pack with a letter showing the full dated timeline.

## The POC Boundary
- Store the renter's records on the device. Pakka makes no network requests and uses no analytics.
- Include an optional passphrase encryption choice, plus delete and export controls.
- Prepare a written confirmation for the renter to send through WhatsApp after tapping. Pakka itself stays offline; WhatsApp is an external app.
- Provide a privacy policy page, terms of service page, and skeleton loading states.
- Generate a move-out pack and letter from the dated evidence timeline.
- Show the last day to give notice, based on the longest notice period the renter logged.

## Explicitly Cut
- Accounts, servers, and sync: the evidence stays on the renter's device.
- Legal advice and lawyer matching: Pakka organizes records; it does not interpret the law or connect renters to lawyers.
