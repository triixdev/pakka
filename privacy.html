Privacy policy

What Pakka keeps

Only what you type or add. That means dates, notes, photos and screenshots.

Where it lives

In your browser on this device. Pakka has no account and no server. We cannot see your data.

What leaves your device

Nothing leaves on its own. When you tap Send as written confirmation your message text goes to WhatsApp so you can send it. Pakka never sends it for you. Your browser may keep that link in its history. There are no cookies and no analytics.

Passphrase

If you lock your record the data is encrypted in your browser. We cannot recover a lost passphrase.

Deleting

Tap Delete everything on the main page. Clearing your browser data does the same.

Hosting

The host of this site may keep ordinary request logs. Those logs never contain your entries.

Questions

Open an issue on the project repository.
