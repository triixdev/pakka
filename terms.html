What Pakka is

A tool to organise your own records about a rented room. It is free and open source under the MIT license.

What Pakka is not

It gives no legal advice. The fingerprint shows a file has not changed since you added it. It does not prove anything in court. Ask a lawyer or a consumer forum about your case.

Your responsibility

Add only records you have the right to keep. Keep your own backups with Export. If you lose your passphrase or clear your browser the data is gone.
